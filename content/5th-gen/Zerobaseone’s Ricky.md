## Bio
Ricky was born Shen Quan Rui in Shanghai, China. As a child, he moved with his family to California. He later moved to Korea to become a trainee with YH Entertainment before participating in BOYS PLANET. He debuted with [[Zerobaseone]] in 2023 after coming 4th. In 2026, he left ZB1 and returned to YH Entertainment with [[Zerobaseone’s Zhang hao|Zhang hao]], [[Zerobaseone’s Gyuvin|Gyuvin]], and [[Zerobaseone’s Yujin|Yujin]], joining EVNNE’s Yoo Seung-eon to form AND2BLE in May 2026.
