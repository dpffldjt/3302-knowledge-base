---
title: 5th Generation
---
In the fifth generation, [[Zerobaseone]] pulls aesthetics and genre from the third generation, capitalising on the nostalgia of dedicated fans from the early years of K-pop’s success in the West, and capturing new audiences as third generation fans become parents in Korea. Meanwhile, [[NewJeans]] latches on to the viral marketing of Tiktok and Y2K nostalgia.
