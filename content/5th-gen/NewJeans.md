## Bio
NewJeans debuted in 2022 under ADOR, sublabel of [[HYBE]]. Before debuting, some members were under other labels, such as Source Music, another HYBE sublabel. In 2021, all members moved to ADOR to be managed and directed personally by Min Hee-jin, well-known for her creative direction of successful [[SM Entertainment]] groups, then CEO of ADOR.

Their debut came as a surprise release of [Attention](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=js1CtxSY38I), followed by smash hit [Hype Boy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11cta61wi0g) and controversial [Cookie](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VOmIplFAGeg). Until 2023, the group released several successful songs, until a legal dispute began in 2024.

In 2024, under the direction of Min Hee-jin, the members requested termination of their contracts with HYBE due to mistreatment in the workplace. This dispute rose all the way to the National Assembly of South Korea, with Hanni testifying to the body. Soon after, Min Hee-jin resigned from ADOR, and the members began working independently of their contracts, including outright support of political protests against then-president Yoon and performing under the name NJZ at a festival in Hong Kong, for which they received an injunction.

In late 2025, Haerin and Hyein returned to ADOR under their original contracts, with Hanni following soon after. Along with the announcement of Hanni’s return, ADOR announced the official termination of Danielle’s contract. As of May 2026, Minji is still in talks with ADOR, but she appeared in group content in July.
