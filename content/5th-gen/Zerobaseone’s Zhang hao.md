## Bio
Zhang Hao was born in Nanping, Fujian, China. He joined YH Entertainment as a trainee before participating in BOYS PLANET. He ended the show rank 1, the first non-Korean participant in a major survival show to do so, debuting in [[Zerobaseone]] in 2023. In 2026, Zhang hao left ZB1 with the other members YH Entertainment, namely [[Zerobaseone’s Ricky|Ricky]], [[Zerobaseone’s Gyuvin|Gyuvin]], and [[Zerobaseone’s Yujin|Yujin]] to form AND2BLE. The group debuted in May 2026 with Zhang Hao as leader.
