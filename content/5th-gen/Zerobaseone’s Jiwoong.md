## Bio
Jiwoong joined BOYS PLANET after several failed attempts at debuting as an idol. In the meantime, he pivoted to acting, best known for his role in *Roommates of Poongduck 304*. 

Though he is currently under Nest Management, Jiwoong was previously a trainee under [[SM Entertainment]]. He has been in several groups before. He was originally in INX as Jinam, and, after their disbandment, he was in the lineup of ATEEN, followed by B.I.T, as King, but neither of these groups made it to debut. He additionally participated in survival show Burn Up, placing first and preparing for debut, but it was cancelled due to COVID restrictions. Finally, Jiwoong joined BOYS PLANET, and debuted in [[Zerobaseone]].
