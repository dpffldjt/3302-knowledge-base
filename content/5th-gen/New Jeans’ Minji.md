## Bio
Before debuting in [[NewJeans]], Minji became a Source Music trainee in 2017, which was then acquired by [[HYBE]] in 2019. In 2021, Minji, along with [[New Jeans’ Hanni|Hanni]], appeared in [[BTS]]’s Permission to Dance music video. Later, Minji was moved from Source Music to ADOR.

New Jeans debuted in 2022, and their 2024 legal dispute has led to Minji still not officially returning to New Jeans, though she participated in group content in mid-2026.
