## Bio
Gunwook joined Jellyfish Entertainment in 2021, originally participating on Wild Idol, though he was eliminated in the last episode and did not debut. He then participated in BOYS PLANET, ultimately debuting in [[Zerobaseone]]. In February 2026, along with [[Zerobaseone’s Matthew|Matthew]], he transferred to Wakeone Entertainment amidst the group’s renewal negotiations.
