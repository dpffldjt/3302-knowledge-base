## Bio
Matthew was born Seok Matthew in Vancouver, Canada. He sometimes uses his Korean name, Woohyun, as well.

Before joining BOYS PLANET, Matthew was a trainee at [[HYBE]] Japan, and he remains close with several members of &Team to this day, particularly Maki. He was briefly under Cube Entertainment in 2022, where he met [[Zerobaseone’s Hanbin|Hanbin]],<sup>1</sup> before moving to MNH Entertainment. He transferred to Wakeone Entertainment in February 2026 during renewal talks with [[Zerobaseone]].
