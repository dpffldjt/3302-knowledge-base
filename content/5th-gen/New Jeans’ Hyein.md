## Bio
Hyein was in kids group U.SSO Girl with Baby Monster’s Rora as U.Jeong from 2017-2018. She was also a child model, and was in Play With Me Club from 2019 until 2021. After debuting with [[NewJeans]] in 2022, she featured on IU’s song [Shh..](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDQTyNmkN4).
