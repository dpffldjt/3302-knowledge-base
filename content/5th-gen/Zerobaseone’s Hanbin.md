## Bio
Hanbin originally trained at Cube Entertainment, during which time he was a backup dancer for Wanna One at SBS Gayo Daejeon in 2018 and for [[BTS]] at the 2019 MMAs.

He debuted with [[Zerobaseone]] as leader after participating in BOYS PLANET. Since his debut, he featured on the soundtrack for *My Lovely Liar* and has been a long-term MC for MCountdown.
