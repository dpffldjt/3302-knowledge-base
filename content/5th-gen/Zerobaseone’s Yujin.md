## Bio
Yujin joined YH Entertainment around 2021 when he was 14 years old. He participated in BOYS PLANET, ending at rank 9 and debuting in [[Zerobaseone]] in 2023. During his time in ZB1, he was an MC for Inkigayo, sharing the stage with [[IVE’s Leeseo]] and [[New Jeans’ Haerin]].

In March 2026, ZB1’s contract ended, and Yujin returned to YH Entertainment to debut as AND2BLE with [[Zerobaseone’s Zhang hao|Zhang hao]], [[Zerobaseone’s Ricky|Ricky]], and [[Zerobaseone’s Gyuvin|Gyuvin]], along with EVNNE’s Yoo Seung-eon.
