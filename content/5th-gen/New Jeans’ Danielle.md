## Bio
Danielle was born Danielle Marsh in Newcastle, New South Wales, Australia, to a white Australian father and a Korean mother. She moved to Korea when she was young and participated in shows like *Rainbow Kindergarten*, *My Heart’s Crayon*, *Shinhwa Broadcasting*, and *Jesse’s Play Kitchen*. Her family moved back to Australia when she was old enough to start school.

Danielle returned to Korea to train with [[YG Entertainment]], then moving to Source Music in 2020. With the other [[NewJeans]] members, she moved to ADOR in 2021, debuting in 2022. During her time in New Jeans, she had writing credits on several of their songs, and she dubbed the Korean version of Disney’s *Ariel* (2023). 

In 2024, after the group got into a legal dispute with ADOR, Danielle and her mother were found to be more involved with conspiring to breach their contracts, so Danielle’s contract with [[HYBE]] was officially terminated in December 2025.
