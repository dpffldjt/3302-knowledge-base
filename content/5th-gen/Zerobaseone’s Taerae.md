## Bio
Before joining BOYS PLANET, Taerae was a trainee at [[SM Entertainment]]. In 2022, he moved to Wakeone Entertainment. Since debuting with [[Zerobaseone]], Taerae has participated in several soundtracks, including smash hit *Queen of Tears*, as well as *Curtain Up, Class* with [[Zerobaseone’s Matthew|Matthew]] and [[Zerobaseone’s Gunwook|Gunwook]].
