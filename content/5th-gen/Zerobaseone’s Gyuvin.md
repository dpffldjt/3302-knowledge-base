## Bio
Gyuvin joined YH Entertainment nearly four years before starting BOYS PLANET. During that time, he became close with [[Zerobaseone’s Yujin|Yujin]], who he later debuted with in [[Zerobaseone]] in 2023. When ZB1’s contract ended, he returned to YH Entertainment with [[Zerobaseone’s Zhang hao|Zhang hao]], [[Zerobaseone’s Ricky|Ricky]], and Yujin. The four of them along with EVNNE’s Yoo Seung-eon became AND2BLE in May 2026.
