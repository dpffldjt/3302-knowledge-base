## Bio
Haerin joined Source Music in 2020. In 2021, she moved to ADOR to join the trainees preparing to become [[NewJeans]]. After debuting in 2022, she hosted Inkigayo’s concert with ATEEZ’s Yunho and [[Zerobaseone’s Yujin]].

After the group’s legal dispute with ADOR, Haerin, along with [[New Jeans’ Hyein]], were the first members to return under their original contracts.
