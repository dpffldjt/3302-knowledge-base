## Bio
Hanni was born Hanni Pham to Vietnamese parents in Melbourne, Australia. She was part of Aemina Dance Crew in Australia. 

After debuting with [[NewJeans]], Hanni participated in writing [Hype Boy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11cta61wi0g) and [OMG](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_ZAgIHmHLdc).
