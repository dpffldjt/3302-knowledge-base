## Bio
Zerobaseone, also known as ZB1, debuted as a nine-member temporary group in 2023. They began on the survival show BOYS PLANET, and are managed as a group under WAKEONE Entertainment. Notably, they are the first survival show group to have a non-Korean member, Zhang hao, rank first.

In their first year, ZB1 achieve a rookie Grand Slam, winning all rookie awards at the end-of-year award shows. Their debut album, [Youth in the Shade](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kkLdQTapgY0VjMajJCSM76A3dxK7EpwCQ), broke the record for K-pop debut album sales. This album is representative of the return to [[3rd-gen/index|3rd generation]] musical styles typical of the [[5th-gen/index|5th generation]].

In March 2024, they made their Japanese debut with [Yura Yura](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xx8qT6H8bkM). They also featured on the soundtrack for Pokemon Horizons: The Series with [Only One Story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9guUZL1Yuv8) later that same year. 

In March 2026, after a three-month extension of their temporary contract, Zhang hao, Ricky, Gyuvin, and Yujin left the group and joined EVNNE’s Yoo Seung-eon as AND2BLE.
