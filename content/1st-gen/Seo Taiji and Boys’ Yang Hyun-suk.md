## Bio
Before being recruited for [[Seo Taiji and Boys]], Hyun-suk was in dance crews Spark and Park Nam Jung and Friends.

In 1996, when Seo Taiji and Boys disbanded, he founded [[YG Entertainment]]. In that time, he had a hand in the creative direction of many successful groups, like [[BigBang]], [[2NE1]], and [[Blackpink]]. During this time, he worked closely with people like Park Jinyoung, founder of JYP Entertainment, for events like the [[Stray Kids]] survival show, as the two judged the JYP vs. YG battle episode.<sup>2</sup>

When the [[Burning Sun scandal]] broke in 2019, people began digging into the histories of all related parties, and Hyun-suk was implicated for unrelated incidents of threatening trainees to make false statements to the police. Due to the backlash, he stepped down from all his roles at YG Entertainment, and his brother stepped down as CEO.

In 2022, Hyun-suk was acquitted for threatening witnesses, and he was newly charged with abuse of power in 2023.<sup>1</sup> Amidst this, he returned to YG Entertainment to take more of a role in creative direction for new girl group Baby Monster.
