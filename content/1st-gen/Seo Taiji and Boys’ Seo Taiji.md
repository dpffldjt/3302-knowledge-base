## Bio
Seo Taiji was born Jung Hyun-cheol in 1972. In 1989, he joined heavy metal band Sinawe.<sup>1</sup> He formed [[Seo Taiji and Boys]] after dropping out of high school to pursue music full time, acting as the leader and main songwriter of the group.<sup>2</sup>

After Seo Taiji and Boys disbanded, Seo Taiji had his solo debut in 1998, releasing a [rock album](https://open.spotify.com/album/1qj5HvUkkUNQ7xwIYYykRu?si=Sp4VT4GDSLmAI86jf90REA), which is largely the genre he would stick with for the rest of his career. In 2000, he returned to Korea for the first time since the group’s disbandment to release [another album](https://open.spotify.com/album/0rKaZNQtWKT3AiFBBSKWkG?si=ej_SPPkdQJeIQ_qZEED4qw), which caused significant dips in popularity for H.O.T and other idol groups gaining momentum at the time. He again disappeared from Korea in 2005, returning in 2008. At that point, he used the speculation about his whereabouts and private life to promote his [next album](https://open.spotify.com/album/2wxrISqeqR1M26Qu3v7qxX?si=3J9OBN1pRZeYjCm8STiO1Q) with the series “Missing Taiji.” 

In 2007, Seo Taiji commemorated his 15th debut anniversary with a limited edition “best of” album of his own work, printing only 15,000 copies. He bought the first 15 himself, keeping #1 and giving #2 and #3 to [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Lee Juno|Lee Juno]] and [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Yang Hyun-suk|Yang Hyun-suk]].

He generally kept out of the spotlight for the next few years, though scandal broke in 2011 when his secret marriage to actress Lee Ji-ah in 1997 was revealed, though the two had been separated since 2000 and divorced in 2006. <sup>3</sup>
