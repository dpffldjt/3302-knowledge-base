## Bio
Fin.K.L debuted under DSP Media in 1998 with four members. Their name means Fin Killing Liberty, however the English name was created to fit the Korean name, 핑클 (pingkeul), chosen by a poll. The group was created to rival [[SM Entertainment]]’s breakout girl group S.E.S.

[[Fin.K.L’s Joohyun|Ock Joohyun]] was the first member found, and she invited her friend [[Fin.K.L’s Jin|Lee Jin]] to join her. [[Fin.K.L’s Yuri|Sung Yuri]] was found next, scouted on a school field trip. The original fourth member was Annie Lee, who left shortly, later joining Tashannie with Yoon Mirae. [[Fin.K.L’s Hyori|Lee Hyori]] was chosen to replace her, and they debuted soon after she joined.<sup>1</sup>

From 1998 to 2002, they released music, going on unofficial hiatus from 2002 to 2005, when they released a [single album](https://open.spotify.com/album/08u61FSaRTXKe6CD7RePvp?si=WzDs-7agSvGGpYiQnvtF4g). They were then inactive until 2019, when the members came back for their [20 year reunion project]([https://open.spotify.com/album/2q89eNLzB43ZPxV2AdFKff?si=CTIt5r-5Q2eQE0NTOhTT3g](https://open.spotify.com/album/1Qwyg4hhC6gv8x42kq9Qru?si=gDmimqxwQWyyzM8a5ZsD-A)). They have been inactive as a group since then, though they have never officially disbanded.
