## Bio
Joohyun was the first member to join [[Fin.K.L]], and she later recruited her friend [[Fin.K.L’s Jin|Jin]] to join her. 

After Fin.K.L’s disbandment, she released several solo albums, and she became a musical actress. She starred in several Korean adaptations of popular musicals, including originating Elphaba in the Korean version of Wicked.
