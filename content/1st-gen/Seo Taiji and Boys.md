## Bio
After other artistic ventures didn’t take off, [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Seo Taiji|Seo Taiji]], along with dancers [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Yang Hyun-suk|Yang Hyun-suk]] and [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Lee Juno|Lee Juno]], created what is considered the first K-pop style musical group in 1992. Foundational characteristics of K-pop as a genre, such as including rap and social critique, originate from this group. The group pulled influences from American music genres like new jack swing in songs like [난 알아요 (I Know)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OEDHEzs5kyk)<sup>1</sup> and mixed elements from traditional Korean music with Western genres in [하여가 Hayeoga](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nXJcDtPj1VM). In 1995, they released [Come Back Home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRFfPZQeJuo), which integrated influences from American gangsta rap.<sup>2</sup> This was later [covered by](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vJFn10XLQM) [[BTS]] for Seo Taiji and Boys’ 25th anniversary project.
