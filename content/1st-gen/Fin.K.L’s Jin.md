## Bio
Jin joined [[Fin.K.L]] before their debut in 1998 when [[Fin.K.L’s Joohyun|Joohyun]] suggested she join. After their unofficial disbandment in 2005, she largely focused on acting, both in scripted and unscripted content. She was a main cast member on *Heroes* in 2010 along with soloist IU, and in 2013 she starred in *The Secret of Birth* with fellow Fin.K.L member [[Fin.K.L’s Yuri|Yuri]]. At present, her acting career is managed by King Kong by Starship, a subsidiary of Starship Entertainment.
