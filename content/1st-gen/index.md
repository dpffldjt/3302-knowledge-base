title: 1st Generation
---
The first generation is marked by the start of “boy groups” and “girl groups” in Korea, with [[Seo Taiji and Boys]] being the first to achieve mainstream success outside of the trot genre. Later, girl groups like S.E.S and [[Fin.K.L]] created competition within the emergent genre.
