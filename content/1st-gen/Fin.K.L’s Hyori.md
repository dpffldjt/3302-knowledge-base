## Bio
Hyori debuted with [[Fin.K.L]] in 1998 shortly after joining the group. She had been scouted by [[SM Entertainment]] and cast by H.O.T’s manager when DSP’s agent found her, and ultimately she joined DSP.<sup>1</sup>

During Fin.K.L’s hiatus in 2003, she had her [solo debut](https://open.spotify.com/album/2uTwUONoLUhlpM6r324B2b?si=fAGIf0qDQOaQS3zxfrvPXg), which exploded in popularity, and the term “Hyori Syndrome” was coined that same year. She had her acting debut in 2005, and she transferred to Mnet Entertainment at this peak in 2006. By 2008, she was hosting *Happy Together* with massively famous Yoo Jaesuk. 

In 2009, Hyori moved to Gil Entertainment, which was owned and run by one of Fin.K.L’s managers, and she stayed there until 2013. In 2010, she released H-Logic, which featured [[BigBang’s Daesung]], 4Minute’s Jiyoon, and After School’s Bekah. However, this album is difficult to come by, as she admitted to plagiarism allegations and production was quickly halted. In 2013, she released Monochrome with the title track [Bad Girls](https://open.spotify.com/track/2IrmdHJjwBH1pFIMcTJ1xq?si=1d258d10c4a04441), and at the end of the year she performed at SBS Gayo Daejeon with [[2NE1’s CL]], who had released The Baddest Female.

In late 2016, Hyori signed with Kiwi Entertainment, which was followed by her show *Hyori’s Homestay* a few months later. This show starred Hyori and her husband hosting people in her home on Jeju Island, starring IU in season 1 and SNSD’s YoonA and actor Park Bogum in season 2 as staff.

She moved to Esteem Entertainment in 2020, creating summer project group SSAK3 with Rain and Yoo Jaesuk as well as project group Refund Sisters with Mamamoo’s Hwasa and soloists Jessi and Uhm Jung-hwa. The following year, she was the first woman to host MAMA solo. Most recently, Hyori signed to Antenna in 2023.
