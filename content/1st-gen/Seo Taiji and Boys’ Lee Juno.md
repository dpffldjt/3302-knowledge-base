## Bio
Lee Juno was recruited by [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Seo Taiji|Seo Taiji]] to join [[Seo Taiji and Boys]] because he was well-known at the time as one of the top dancers in Korea.<sup>1</sup>

After Seo Taiji and Boys’ disbandment, Juno generally kept out of the public eye. This privacy did not last forever, as he was charged with fraud and indecent acts in 2013. Later, he was charged with harassment in 2016, and he was found guilty of both charges. [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Yang Hyun-suk|Yang Hyun-suk]] paid his debts and petitioned for him in court at these trials.<sup>2</sup>
