## Bio
Yuri was born Sung Yuri in 1981 in West Germany, where her father was a university professor. She joined DSP Media and [[Fin.K.L]] when she was scouted on a school field trip. 

During Fin.K.L’s hiatus in 2002, Yuri had her acting debut in *Bad Girls*. After Fin.K.L’s small reunion in 2005, Yuri left DSP Media and joined SidusHQ. In 2008, she starred in *Hong Gil-dong*, which was the project that made the public take her seriously as an actress. In 2011, Yuri  moved to King Kong by Starship, subsidiary of Starship Entertainment. 

She continued her acting career through 2013, when she starred in *The Secret of Birth* with [[Fin.K.L’s Jin|Jin]]. The next year, she moved to Fantagio Entertainment, then signing with SL Entertainment in 2015, who manage her to this day.
