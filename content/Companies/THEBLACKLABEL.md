THEBLACKLABEL was established as a subsidiary of YG Entertainment. More recently, it has become an independent label with YG as a smaller shareholder instead. 

In 2025, K-pop Demon Hunters producer worked with THEBLACKLABEL to produce the music for the movie, taking significant inspiration from [[Blackpink]]’s music and branding.
