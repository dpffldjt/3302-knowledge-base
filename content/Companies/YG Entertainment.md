After [[Seo Taiji and Boys]]’ disbandment in 1996, [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Yang Hyun-suk|Yang Hyun-suk]] founded YG Entertainment. As CCO, he took part in the creative direction of groups like BigBang, 2NE1, Blackpink, and, currently, Baby Monster.
