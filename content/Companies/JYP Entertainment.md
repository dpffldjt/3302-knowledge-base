JYP Entertainment was founded by Park Jinyoung (J.Y.Park) and Bang Si-hyuk of [[HYBE]] in 1997. In the early days, he oversaw g.o.d in partnership with SidusHQ. The company’s first girl group, the Wonder Girls, found success both in Korea and abroad, leading to further ventures, like the show *Hot Blood Men*, which eventually led to the debuts of 2PM and 2AM, the latter of which was jointly managed with [[BigHit Music]]. 

JYP has long been considered one of the “big three” (now big four) entertainment companies in Korea, debuting groups like Twice and Got7 in the [[3rd-gen/index|3rd generation]], and [[Stray Kids]] and NMIXX in the [[4th-gen/index|4th]].
