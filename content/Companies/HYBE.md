title: HYBE
date: 2005-02-04
---
Bang Si-hyuk founded HYBE in 2005 after his success in co-founding [[JYP Entertainment]]. HYBE was originally called BigHit Entertainment, but rebranded in 2021 when they went public. [[BigHit Music]] is a private sublabel of HYBE using its old name and branding.

HYBE started with girl group GLAM, who disbanded after one of the members was put in prison for blackmail. In a last attempt to save the company, [[BTS]] debuted. Since then, HYBE acquired other labels, like Source Music and ADOR, and the name BigHit was moved to a private sublabel under HYBE itself.
