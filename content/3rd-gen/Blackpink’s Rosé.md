## Bio
Rosé was born Roseanne Park in 1997. Though she was born in Auckland, Aotearoa, she grew up in Melbourne, Australia for most of her life, moving to Korea when she became a trainee at [[YG Entertainment]].

Before debuting with [[Blackpink]], Rosé featured on [Without You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bKSjRJhXwo) by [[BigBang’s G-Dragon]], initially anonymously, then credited after her debut.

In 2021, Rosé made her solo debut [-R-](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K9_VFxzCuQ0&list=PL01yu2j2OZBblvhpeaL6IN0t-MD5NquMB&index=6). She signed with [[THEBLACKLABEL]], a subsidiary of YG, for her solo activities in 2024. That same year, she released another solo album, with [a feature by Bruno Mars](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ekr2nIex040).
