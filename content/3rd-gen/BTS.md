## Bio
BTS debuted in 2013 under [[BigHit Music]]. BTS initially stood for ”Bangtan Sonyeondan” (방탄소년단), meaning ‘bulletproof boy scouts’ in English, but this was changed in 2017 to “Beyond the Scene.”

BTS have become the best known Korean group globally, and perhaps the biggest boy band since the Beatles. As such, they’ve broken several records held for decades by the Beatles and the Jackson 5. Similarly, they were the first Korean group to earn Gold and Platinum certifications by the RIAA.

Coming from a small entertainment company in Korea, BTS did not have immediate success. For their first few years, they gradually grew their fanbase, until their break into the US market in 2017, when they won the Top Social Artist award at the Billboard Music Awards. That same year, [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Seo Taiji|Seo Taiji]] chose them to cover [Come Back Home](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vJFn10XLQM) for [[Seo Taiji and Boys]]’ 25th anniversary. In 2018, they were awarded the Order of Cultural Merit by President Moon, a mark of their impact on Korean cultural exports.

They’ve since exploded in popularity both in Korean and internationally, releasing their first full-English single, [Dynamite](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gdZLi9oWNZg), in 2020, which received a Grammy nomination, making BTS the first Korean artist to do so. In 2022, just after their 9th anniversary, the group announced a hiatus of group activities to fulfill their military duties. During this time, all members released individual projects. They came back together after their 13th anniversary in 2026, releasing their most recent album, [Arirang](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lkejA6kcdE00kf8UisGo0xqiSWHhy9r8s). They also most recently performed at the [halftime show of the 2026 World Cup final](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E3nWu1VgRJU).
