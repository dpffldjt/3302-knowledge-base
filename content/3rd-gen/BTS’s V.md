## Bio
V was born Kim Taehyung in 1995. He became a trainee at [[BigHit Music]] after joining his friend for his audition.

In 2016, V made his acting debut in Hwarang, also taking part in the soundtrack with [[BTS’s Jin|Jin]]. His solo debut was not until 2023 with the album [Layover](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kNOuQThHGWJNos0pyFpQ8OMmsvlB5QeaA), which was followed by his enlistment with [[BTS’s RM|RM]]. In the years leading up to his enlistment, he was rumoured to be in a relationship with [[Blackpink’s Jennie]].
