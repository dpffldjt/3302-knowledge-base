## Bio
Jin was born Kim Seokjin in 1992. While studying to be an actor, Jin was scouted by [[BigHit Music]],<sup>1</sup> and thus joined the company. 

It wasn’t until 2022 that Jin had his solo debut with the single [The Astronaut](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c6ASQOwKkhk), just before his enlistment that same year. As the first member to enlist, he was also the first to come back, being discharged in 2024.
