## Bio
Jennie was born Kim Jennie in 1996. She lived in Auckland, Aotearoa for several years, going by Jennie Ruby Jane, which she sometimes uses in her branding.

Jennie made her solo debut, called [Solo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b73BI9eUkjM), in 2018, shortly after debuting with [[Blackpink]]. 

Unlike other labels, [[YG Entertainment]] is known to have more lax dating rules for its idols. In 2019, she had a brief public relationship with EXO’s Kai. In 2021, Jennie’s year-long relationship with [[BigBang’s G-Dragon]] was revealed.

When contracts were negotiated in 2023, Jennie did not sign with YG for her individual work, instead establishing her own label, ODD ATELIER.
