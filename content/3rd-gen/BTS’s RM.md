## Bio
RM was born Kim Namjoon in 1994. Before becoming a trainee, he was a well-known underground rapper under the name Runch Randa. He joined [[BigHit Music]] in 2010, initially preparing to join a hiphop group. When the concept changed from hiphop group to idol group, RM was the only member of the original lineup to stay on. 

In 2013, he debuted in BTS under the name Rap Monster as leader, rapper, and producer with [[BTS’s J-Hope|J-Hope]] and [[BTS’s Suga|Suga]]. Soon after, in 2015, he released his [first solo mixtape, called RM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0XAxf8aFtL4), a collection of original songs that were not officially released on streaming platforms. In 2017, along with the group’s rebrand to Beyond the Scene, he changed his stage name to RM, now meaning “Real Me.”

RM released his first official solo album, [Indigo](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lnV6Uf8a9g0bz8D_m8rW2_z6bokxA_MR0), in 2022. In 2023, he enlisted with fellow member [[BTS’s V|V]], and was then discharged in 2025.
