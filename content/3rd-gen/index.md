---
title: 3rd Generation
---
The third generation of K-pop is marked by its push into the Western market. This is evident in particularly [[BTS]] and [[Blackpink]]’s career progression and, especially, their English proficiency. While previous generations focused on success in Korea with Western success as a side effect, in this era, groups and their companies worked concurrently on promoting idols in Korea and worldwide.
