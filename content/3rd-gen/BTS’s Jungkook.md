## Bio
Jungkook was born in 1997. Before joining [[BigHit Music]], he was scouted by many other companies. He joined BigHit because he’d heard of [[BTS’s RM|RM]] before from his time as Runch Randa.

His first solo album, [Seven](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QU9c0053UAU), was released in 2023. He enlisted that same year with [[BTS’s Jimin|Jimin]], and he was discharged in 2025. He is also close with [[Stray Kids’ Bang Chan]] and [[Stray Kids’ Lee Know]], who visited him while he was in the military.
