## Bio
Lisa was born Pranpriya Manobal in Buriram Province, Thailand, though she later legally changed her name to Lalisa Manobal. She was [[YG Entertainment]]’s first non-Korean trainee. In Thailand, she was childhood friends with Got7’s Bambam, as they were on the same dance team. In 2013, she appeared in [Ringa Linga](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UJfZ69MSlvY) by [[BigBang’s Taeyang]].

After her debut with [[Blackpink]], she had her solo debut in 2021 with [Lalisa](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=awkkyBH2zEo). A few years later, in 2024, she established her own label, Lloud, to manage her solo activities while staying with YG for Blackpink’s group activities. The next year, she had both her acting debut, in The White Lotus, and her first solo album release, [ALTER EGO](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kmGTbkuCdlDMn7AJLY0fo5mTKRKaX8CSQ).
