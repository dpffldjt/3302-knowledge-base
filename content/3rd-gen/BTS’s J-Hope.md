## Bio
J-Hope was born in 1994 as Jung Hoseok. Before joining [[BTS]], he auditioned for [[JYP Entertainment]], but he ended up joining [[BigHit Music]] instead. Soon after, he appeared as a rapper in labelmate JoKwon’s [Animal](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tp2o4_8ZVqI).

In 2013, he debuted with BTS as a rapper and producer along with members [[BTS’s RM|RM]] and [[BTS’s Suga|Suga]]. His first solo mixtape, [Daydream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OK3GJ0WIQ8s), came out in 2018. His official solo debut was in 2022 with [Jack in the Box](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_k2PoHazK5q2CkhQ7Nrk2nqqqGAfdLl9qU). Shortly after, in 2023, he enlisted in the military, coming back in 2024.
