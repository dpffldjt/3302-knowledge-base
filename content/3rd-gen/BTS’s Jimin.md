## Bio
Jimin was born in 1995. He was the last member to join [[BTS]], becoming a trainee at [[BigHit Music]] in 2012, one year before they debuted. 

In 2023, he collaborated with [[BigBang’s Taeyang]] on [VIBE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M5Q0lLBj1ig). That same year, he released his first solo album, [Face](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kfk5qQMVWGQHFm6aKsm98uJeNjJRSa7uo), and enlisted in the military with [[BTS’s Jungkook|Jungkook]]. He was discharged in 2025.
