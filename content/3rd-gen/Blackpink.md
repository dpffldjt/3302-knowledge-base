## Bio
Blackpink debuted in 2016 with four members under [[YG Entertainment]]. They were meant to be “the next [[2NE1]],” as the label’s second girl group, debuting right around when 2NE1 disbanded. 

Since they debuted, they’ve risen quickly in popularity in Korea and abroad. In 2018, just two years after their debut, [Ddu-Du Ddu-Du](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IHNzOHi8sJs) debuted on the Billboard Hot 100, and it was the first K-pop group video to pass 1 billion views on YouTube, marking their success internationally. In 2019, they became the first Korean girl group to perform at Coachella.

In 2023, all four members renewed with YG Entertainment for group activities, but split up for their solo work. 

Their impact can be seen, like [[IVE]], in their influence on the soundtrack of Netflix hit K-pop Demon Hunters, as songwriter EJAE has cited Blackpink as one of her main influences.
