## Bio
Suga was born Min Yoongi in 1993. He joined [[BigHit Music]] second, after [[BTS’s RM|RM]]. Like RM, he was well-known in his hometown, Daegu, as a rapper before becoming a trainee, going by the name Gloss. In 2010, he produced [518-062](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mfim-5l_AQ4) with D-Town.

As a [[BTS]] member, he produced most of the group’s catalog, along with fellow rappers RM and [[BTS’s J-Hope]]. In 2016, he released his first solo mixtape, [AgustD](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Y_Eiyg4bfk). For the following few years, he produced for BTS and did projects with other artists, including production and features on their songs. In 2020, Suga released his second mixtape, [D-2](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lV1BhQbKMjudkNoNKYokycJgbbWDdt7ZM), featuring RM. That same year, he released his first collaboration with IU.

Over the next few years, Suga collaborated with other artists many times, and in 2023 he released his official first album, [D-Day](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_n-oXwLYZMPpGJhLjVxGoWgm-qeRmpV428), including features by [[BTS’s J-Hope|J-Hope]] and IU. That year, he enlisted in the military, coming back just after BTS’s 12th anniversary in 2025.
