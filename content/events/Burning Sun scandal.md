## Overview
The Burning Sun scandal was a high-profile criminal scandal involving many well-known figures in Korea. [[BigBang’s Seungri]] became the face of the incident in the news, as he was the most famous person involved, though he may not have been the most involved of the perpetrators. Fellow musician Jung Joonyoung was charged and imprisoned for 5 years. Other idols implicated were Choi Jonghoon, formerly of F.T. Island, soloists Roy Kim and Eddy Kim, Yong Junhyung, formerly of Highlight, and Lee Jonghyun, formerly of CNBLUE, of which only Choi Jonghoon was convicted of any crime.
