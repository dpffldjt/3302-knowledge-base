## Bio
Seungmin joined [[JYP Entertainment]] in 2017 along with [[Stray Kids’ Lee Know|Lee Know]]. Since [[Stray Kids]]’ debut, he has taken part in several TV soundtracks, and he has independently released covers and solo songs on YouTube.
