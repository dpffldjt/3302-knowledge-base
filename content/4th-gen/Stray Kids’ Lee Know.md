## Bio
Lee Know was born Lee Minho in 1998. His stage name is Lee Know in an effort to differentiate himself from mega-famous actor Lee Minho, a household name in Korea. Lee Know joined [[JYP Entertainment]] only a few months before [[Stray Kids]]’ debut, becoming one of the fastest trainees to pass JYP’s famously rigorous dance program due to his experience as a dancer. Before joining JYP, Lee Know had toured with [[BTS]] as a backup dancer on their Wings tour. He remains close with BTS members to this day, having visited [[BTS’s Jungkook]] during his enlistment.
