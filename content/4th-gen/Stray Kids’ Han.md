## Bio
Han was born Han Jisung in 2000. He spent much of his childhood outside Korea in Malaysia, so when he joined [[JYP Entertainment]] in 2014, he spent more time with the foreign trainees, including [[Stray Kids’ Bang Chan|Bang Chan]]. He and Bang Chan, along with [[Stray Kids’ Changbin|Changbin]], formed 3Racha in 2016, releasing music as a unit before [[Stray Kids]] is formed in 2017. 

Since their debut, Han has released solo music on YouTube and participated in drama soundtracks both alone and with other members.
