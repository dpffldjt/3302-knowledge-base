## Bio
Wonyoung debuted with IZ\*ONE in 2018 after participating in Produce 101. After IZ\*ONE’s disbandment in 2021, she debuted in [[IVE]]. 

While in IVE, Wonyoung has become a very popular MC, hosting Music Bank for 16 months and the AAAs annually. She hosted the 7th AAAs with Super Junior’s Leeteuk, the 8th with soloist Kang Daniel, the 9th with [[Zerobaseone’s Hanbin]], the 10th with 2PM’s Junho, and she will host the 11th AAAs in December 2026.

Wonyoung is well-known in Korea for her many brand endorsements, to the extent that she and [[Stray Kids’ Felix]] won the “Presence of K-pop Award” at the AAAs in 2025.
