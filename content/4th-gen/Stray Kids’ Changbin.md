## Bio
Changbin joined [[JYP Entertainment]] in 2016 along with [[Stray Kids’ Hyunjin|Hyunjin]] and [[Stray Kids’ I.N|I.N]], forming 3Racha that same year with [[Stray Kids’ Bang Chan|Bang Chan]] and [[Stray Kids’ Han|Han]]. Changbin is also known to be close with ATEEZ’s Wooyoung, who was also born in 1999.

In 2023, Changbin became well-known in Korea for his ambassadorship for Samsung, releasing [Fly High](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n4ErkvjcuVM), followed by featuring on [8th Grader (Respect Your Dreams)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tIudkczF3_I) by Kim Chang-wan as promotion for the Galaxy phone line.
