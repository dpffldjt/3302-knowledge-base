## Bio
I.N was born Yang Jeongin in 2001. He joined [[JYP Entertainment]] in 2016 along with [[Stray Kids’ Changbin|Changbin]] and [[Stray Kids’ Hyunjin|Hyunjin]]. 

Since his debut, he has released solo music on YouTube and participated in drama soundtracks, as well as becoming a global ambassador for Damiani.
