## Bio
Before his debut with [[Stray Kids]], Woojin trained at [[SM Entertainment]] with some of the NCT members. He eventually moved to [[JYP Entertainment]] in 2017 before being picked for the Stray Kids survival show. He left Stray Kids and JYP in 2019, citing personal reasons. 

In 2021, Woojin made his solo debut [The moment: 未成年 a minor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OMDWttpL2bw). In 2023, he made his acting debut in Brazilian HBO series *Além do Guarda-Roupa*, and he has since been in several stage shows in Korea as well. He moved from 10x to Kustomade in 2024, a subsidiary of SM Entertainment. In 2025, Woojin enlisted in the military.
