## Bio
Yujin debuted in IZ\*ONE in 2018 after participating in Produce 101. IZ\*ONE then disbanded in mid-2021, and Yujin joined [[IVE]], debuting as leader later that year.

During her time with IVE, Yujin has done many solo projects. She hosted Inkigayo for a time with Kang Daniel and RIIZE’s Sungchan, and she later hosted Gayo Daejeon with Shinee’s Key and Astro’s Cha Eunwoo. Along with this, she participated in *Earth Arcade* with Oh My Girl’s Mimi and soloist Lee Youngji. For Gatorade, she collaborated with soloist Kang Daniel and Olympic figure skater Kim Yuna on [Move Like This](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EkiMQaWc5ZI). She also sang the Korean dub of Disney Wish’s [This Wish](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQpbzrKbosE).
