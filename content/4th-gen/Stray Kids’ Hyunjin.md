## Bio
Hyunjin joined [[JYP Entertainment]] in 2016 along with [[Stray Kids’ Changbin|Changbin]] and [[Stray Kids’ I.N|I.N]]. He became the first Korean celebrity to be a global ambassador for Versace, and he is known for being close to Donatella Versace.
