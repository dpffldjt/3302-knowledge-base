## Bio
IVE debuted in 2021 with six members under Starship Entertainment.
Two members, Wonyoung and Yujin, were previously in temporary group IZ\*ONE with Le Sserafim’s Sakura and Chaewon and soloists Kwon Eunbi, Jo Yuri, Choi Yena, and Lee Chaeyeon.

They quickly cemented their place as the most popular girl group in the [[4th-gen/index|4th generation]], performing the send-off for the South Korean Olympic delegation only months after their debut. Since their debut, they’ve achieved multiple triple crowns, winning all three major music shows in a week, and perfect all-kills, topping the daily, weekly, and real-time charts in Korea. Wonyoung has become a household name in Korea, as she and [[Stray Kids’ Felix]] are respectively the male and female idols who appear in the most advertisements domestically.

IVE, like [[Blackpink]], notably influenced the soundtrack of massive success K-pop Demon Hunters, with their song [I Am](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6ZUIwj3FgUY) inspiring much of [Golden](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yebNIHKAC4A).
