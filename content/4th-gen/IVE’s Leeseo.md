## Bio
Leeseo was born Lee Hyun-seo in 2007. After debuting with [[IVE]], she hosted Inkigayo from April 2024 to December 2025, with cohosts actor Moon Seonghyun, [[Zerobaseone’s Yujin]], TWS’s Shinyu, and &Teams’s EJ.
