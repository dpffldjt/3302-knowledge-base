## Bio
Gaeul was originally cast by [[JYP Entertainment]], later joined Starship Entertainment, then debuted in [[IVE]] in late 2021. She has participated in the development of their songs, such as writing for songs like [Kitsch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pG6iaOMV46I) and creating choreography for [♥beats](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhdAQWY9_JY) and [TKO](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cxFF3FKyp5g).
