## Bio
Liz was born Kim Jiwon in 2004. Before debuting in [[IVE]], Liz appeared in Taeyeon’s [This Christmas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hsh54g9JmC0) music video.

She collaborated with Idle’s Soyeon and Aespa’s Winter on [Nobody](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N68rwFjyinI) in 2023. In 2024, she participated in the OST for *Love Your Enemy*, and then in 2025 in *Heavenly Ever After*.
