---
title: 4th Generation
---
After the mainstream commercial success of the third generation, groups like [[Stray Kids]] and Idle could take risks, self-producing and making more experimental music. At the same time, groups like [[IVE]] gain popularity, combining the group’s concept with outside partnerships into one cohesive concept.
