## Bio
Felix was born Lee Felix in Sydney, Australia, in 2000. His Korean name is Lee Yongbok. He auditioned for and was accepted into [[JYP Entertainment]] in 2016, but he stayed in Australia until 2017 to complete his schooling. When he became a trainee, he was at risk of being cut due to his Korean skills, so [[Stray Kids’ Lee Know|Lee Know]] tutored him during the survival show.

Since [[Stray Kids]]’ debut, Felix has become famous in Korea for his brand partnerships, and he can be seen on advertisements everywhere in Korea. He also has partnerships with brands like Louis Vuitton globally. As such, at the 2025 AAAs, he won the “Presence of K-pop Award” along with [[IZONE’s Wonyoung]].
