## Bio
Stray Kids debuted under [[JYP Entertainment]] in 2018 after being formed on a survival show by the same name in 2017. Bang Chan personally chose the nine original members, including himself, and was involved in much of the group’s conceptualisation, as the most senior trainee in the group. Stray Kids are part of the growing number of groups in the [[4th-gen/index|4th generation]] which have high involvement from members in production and ideation.

They released their first album, [Mixtape](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mMJWqiNVdYGmxFpVKZlFtyjqRSQ44dRCg), before their official debut, as part of their survival show promotion. In late 2019, Woojin left the group, citing personal reasons. Their breakout success came in 2020 with [God’s Menu](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TQTlCHxyuu8), which became a viral hit in Korea. They have steadily grown in popularity in Korea and in the West. Like [[Blackpink]], they performed at Coachella, and member Felix competes with [[IZONE’s Wonyoung|Jang Wonyoung from IVE and IZONE]] for most brand partnerships in Korea.
