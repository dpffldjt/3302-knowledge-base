## Bio
Bang Chan was born Christopher Chahn Bahng in 1997. Though he was born in South Korea, he moved to Sydney, Australia, after just a few months. He joined [[JYP Entertainment]] in 2010 after auditioning, thus moving away from his family, who stayed in Australia. While a trainee, he prepared to debut with Got7, but was removed from the lineup before their debut. He remains close with other idols born in ‘97, including Got7’s Bambam and Yugyeom, [[BTS’s Jungkook]], and [[Blackpink’s Lisa]]. Also while training at JYP, he became close with other English speaking idols, like Day6’s Young K and NMIXX’s Lily. 

Pre-debut, Bang Chan was highly involved with other JYP artists’ projects, including cameos in TWICE’s [Like Ooh-Ahh](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0rtV5esQT6I) and Miss A’s [Only You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zO9RzrhYR-I) music videos. In 2016, he, going by the name CB97, and fellow [[Stray Kids]] members [[Stray Kids’ Changbin|Changbin, as SpearB]] and [[Stray Kids’ Han|Han, as J.One]] created 3Racha, which continues as a subunit and production team of the group today. In 2017, they released their [first mixtape](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mMJWqiNVdYGmxFpVKZlFtyjqRSQ44dRCg). Later that year, he chose his team for the Stray Kids survival show, debuting with that team in 2018.

Since their debut, Bang Chan has been the lead producer of Stray Kids’ discography, and he does much of the arrangement and composition as well. With the release of their album [This & That](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=69RS0DXaoO0) in August of 2026, he will become the most credited idol in KOMCA, surpassing VIXX’s Ravi.
