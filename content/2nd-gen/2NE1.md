## Bio
2NE1 debuted in May 2009 and is known for being an experimental group as well as being one of the frontrunners of the Korean Wave. Early on, they were known as the “female [[BigBang]],” debuting soon after them in the same label, [[YG Entertainment]].

2NE1 is meant to mean the Korean pronunciation of 21 in English, as well as “to anyone.” YG also said it represents the 21st century, with NE meaning “New Evolution.”<sup>1</sup> On a similar note, fans are called BlackJacks.

2013 and 2014 were years of major international recognition for 2NE1, including a collaboration with Will.i.am, appearances on *The Bachelor* and *America’s Next Top Model*, and the release of massive hit [I Am the Best](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7_lSP8Vc3o).

In 2015, while the members were doing their own individual activities, the group only appeared together once, for the 2015 MAMA awards, attached to CL’s solo performance. Then, in early 2016, [[2NE1’s Minzy|Minzy]] went on hiatus, and in November of that year it was announced that the group would be disbanding. In 2021, CL and Minzy said they both found out from the press release.<sup>2</sup>

Since their disbandment, the members reunited for Coachella in 2022. [[YG Entertainment]] announced that they would be doing a series of concerts for their 15th anniversary in 2024, and they have been active since then. However, [[2NE1’s Bom|Bom]] has been on medical hiatus since mid-2025.
