## Bio
2NE1 debuted in May 2009 and is known for being an experimental group as well as being one of the frontrunners of the Korean Wave. Early on, they were known as the “female [[BigBang]],” debuting soon after them in the same label, [[YG Entertainment]].

2NE1 is meant to mean the Korean pronunciation of 21 in English, as well as “to anyone.” YG also said it represents the 21st century, with NE meaning “New Evolution.”<sup>1</sup> On a similar note, fans are called BlackJacks.

May 2009-November 2016, 2024-2025
