## Bio
CL was born in 1991 in South Korea, but she spent most of her childhood abroad in France and Japan. Before debuting, she worked with labelmates [[BigBang]] as a feature on one of their earliest singles. In 2009, she debuted with [[2NE1]] as the leader of the group. Around this time, she collaborated with Teddy Park and [[BigBang’s G-Dragon]] on The Leaders.

A few years after debut, CL released her first solo single, [The Baddest Female](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LP4foN3Xs4&pp=ygUSdGhlIGJhZGRlc3QgZmVtYWxl). Her official solo debut didn’t come until 2015, during which time she focused on promotion in the US. In 2018, she performed at the Olympics in Korea, and she made her acting debut in *Mile 22*. 
![[CL at 2018 Olympics.jpg|240]]
CL with Korean president Moon Jae-in, First Lady Kim Jung-sook, and Ivanka Trump<sup>3</sup>

Shortly after, in 2019, CL left [[YG Entertainment]]<sup>1</sup>, who managed her solo career after 2NE1’s disbandment. In 2021, she joined Konnect Entertainment<sup>2</sup>, which manages her to this day.
