## Bio
Born Park Bom in 1984, Bom joined [[YG Entertainment]] in 2006 after years of auditioning. Before debuting with [[2NE1]], she featured on multiple [[BigBang]] singles and collaborated with labelmate Lexy.

Bom debuted with 2NE1 in May 2009, releasing her first solo song later that year. In 2010, Bom also featured on a song with [[BigBang’s G-Dragon|GD]]&[[BigBang’s T.O.P|TOP]], followed by several other collaborations with G-Dragon. In 2013, she joined duo Bom & Hi with LeeHi, also of YG.

In 2014, Bom went into hiatus after a scandal involving her ADHD medication being shipped from the US to Korea, where Adderall is illegal. Afterwards, she performed with 2NE1 at the 2015 MAMA awards, recorded their disbandment single [Goodbye](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wEkLHC7l25w), and was released from her contract.

In April 2017, Bom announced she had signed with [[THEBLACKLABEL]], but the agency denied it. She insisted she had, but in 2018, she signed with D-nation, her current label. She has released an album and several singles with them, including collaborations with Mamamoo’s Wheein, [[BigBang’s Taeyang]], and Pentagon’s Dawn, among others. During this time, she has intermittently worked with [[YG Entertainment]] again, such as a feature on *YG Future Strategy Office*, a Netflix show featuring [[BigBang’s Seungri]]. She also joined the 2NE1 reunion with the other members, though she has been on medical hiatus since mid-2025.
