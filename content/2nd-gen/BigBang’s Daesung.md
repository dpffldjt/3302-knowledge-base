## Bio
Daesung is the youngest member of [[BigBang]], born in 1989. He became the third member of BigBang, after [[BigBang’s G-Dragon|G-Dragon]] and [[BigBang’s Taeyang|Taeyang]]. He debuted with BigBang in 2006, and shortly after he struggled with vocal nodes and stage fright.<sup>1</sup>

Daesung stands out among BigBang members, as his solo work is very popular in Japan, achieving records in Japan previously held only by Michael Jackson.<sup>2</sup> Also, he released early solo music in the trot genre<sup>3</sup>, which diverged heavily from BigBang’s largely hip-hop sound. He now releases most of his solo work in Japanese due to his popularity there. While most idol groups don’t perform their own instrumentals, especially live, some time into BigBang’s career Daesung began playing the drums live in performances, as seen in [Sober](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gkL4SP5bpzQ) from the MADE tour.

In 2022, Daesung left [[YG Entertainment]] but stayed as a member of BigBang. In early 2023, he joined RND Company.
