## Bio
Seungri, born Lee Seung-hyun in 1990, was a member of [[BigBang]] from their debut until his retirement from the entertainment industry. His stage name, Seungri, means “victory” in Korean. While active in the industry, he was involved in BigBang’s group work, his own solo work in Korea and Japan, and starred in musicals and films.

Seungri quickly got into business in several industries after BigBang’s financial success, including investing in Club Burning Sun. Due to his association with the club and his DJing work there, as well as his status as a member of BigBang, much of the press related to the [[Burning Sun incident]] named him, and public opinion turned against him. Soon after, in 2019, Seungri retired from the industry, leaving [[YG Entertainment]] permanently<sup>1</sup>. 

He was convicted of several crimes, including gambling, misappropriation of funds, and mediation of prostitution in military court, where he was tried due to his concurrent enlistment period.<sup>2</sup> Ultimately, he was sentenced to three years in prison and a fine of 1.15 billion won. He was released in 2023 after serving 18 months.<sup>3</sup>
