---
title: 2nd Generation
---
The second generation is the start of the idol system, as companies like [[SM Entertainment]], [[YG Entertainment]], and [[JYP Entertainment]] emerge as the “big three,” training teenagers in dance, singing, and rap while organising their groups for them. YG’s [[BigBang]] and [[2NE1]] are early success stories from this system.
