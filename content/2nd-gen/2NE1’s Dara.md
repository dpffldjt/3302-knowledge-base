## Bio
Dara was born Park Sandara in 1984. In 1990, her brother, who would become MBLAQ’s Thunder, was born. In 1994, her family moved to the Philippines. There, Dara worked to improve her language and musical abilities in hopes of becoming a singer, which she decided on after listening to [[Seo Taiji and Boys]]’ 난 알아요 (I Know).<sup>1</sup> While in the Philippines, she also made her acting debut in 2004 in *Bcuz of U*,<sup>2</sup> as well as releasing her first album, [Sandara](https://www.deezer.com/en/album/442498947). In 2007, she returned to Korea, joining [[YG Entertainment]] after being scouted by [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Yang Hyun-suk]] in 2004 when she was in a documentary.<sup>3</sup>

In 2009, along with debuting in [[2NE1]], she also made her solo debut with [Kiss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZAqiMCp9zrI) featuring [[2NE1’s CL|CL]]. In that same year, she featured on [[BigBang’s G-Dragon]]’s song [Hello](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xeumQGwNFes), and the next year she featured in [[BigBang’s Taeyang]]’s [I Need a Girl](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Ui2-Om1Cu0&pp=ygUaZGFyYSB0YWV5YW5nIGkgbmVlZCBhIGdpcmw%3D) music video.

Dara continued her work in acting and unscripted TV in both the Philippines and Korea, guesting on *Pinoy Big Brother* and acting in web series *What’s eating Steven Yeun*, among other shows, from 2014 to the present, including roles in *Cheese in the Trap* and *Idol League* with BTOB’s Eunkwang.

In 2023, Dara released her first Korean-language solo album, [Sandara Park](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ejdqUXXy1Rc).
