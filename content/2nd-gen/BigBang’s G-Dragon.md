## Bio
G-Dragon, born Kwon Ji Yong, is the leader of [[BigBang]] and member of BigBang subunit [[GD&TOP]]. His stage name comes from his birth name, with 龍 (yong) meaning dragon in Korean. 

In his childhood, he was friends with [[BigBang’s T.O.P]], and was ultimately the reason T.O.P was added to BigBang. At 7, he joined “Little [[Roo’ra]],” but after their sudden disbandment he promised his mother to never try again to become a singer. He then trained for five years, from 8-13, at [[SM Entertainment]].<sup>1</sup> Soon after leaving SM, he joined [[YG Entertainment]] after being scouted by [[Jinusean’s Sean]].<sup>2</sup>

## Solo Work
Outside of his work with BigBang, he has released three solo albums and two EPs.
### Albums
#### Heartbreaker (2009)
Heartbreaker was released on G-Dragon’s 21st birthday, and included features from people like [[2NE1’s Dara]], [[BigBang’s Taeyang|Taeyang]], and [[2NE1’s CL]], who also has a writing credit on the album.
- [A Boy (소년이여)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PMwWxmrQSGE)
- [The Leaders ft. Teddy, CL](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oSzP9tUXXec)
#### Coup d’Etat (2013)
Though it was released in 2013, this album gained renewed success in December 2024, when then-president Yoon Suk Yeol attempted a self-coup, leading to protests in the streets. Popular music at these protests included Crooked, [[SNSD]]’s Into the New World, and [[2NE1]]’s I Am the Best. 
- [COUP D’ETAT (Feat. Diplo & Baauer)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gv7v_ZD5vbA&list=OLAK5uy_mO6VwoSDefKbg_5MjTXKKIK0MXubbAeAg)
- [비딱하게 (Crooked)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ERbDApFPOxE&list=OLAK5uy_mO6VwoSDefKbg_5MjTXKKIK0MXubbAeAg&index=8)
#### Ubermensch (2025)
G-Dragon’s first release since his move to Galaxy Corporation, this album has a feature with Anderson .Paak as well as a track including all remaining BigBang members. Power was also a serendipitous follow-up to the popularity of Crooked from Coup d’Etat, as this album was released only a couple months later.
- [HOME SWEET HOME (feat. TAEYANG & DAESUNG)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fLi0EJfi_vg&list=PLu9-Jw6kolDym2K4g-abqHxKE-5yjC36J&index=1&pp=iAQB8AUB)
- [POWER](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NMjhjrBIrG8&list=PLu9-Jw6kolDym2K4g-abqHxKE-5yjC36J&index=2)
- [TOO BAD (feat. Anderson .Paak)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o9DhvbqYzns&list=PLu9-Jw6kolDym2K4g-abqHxKE-5yjC36J&index=3)
### EPs
#### One of a Kind (2012)
On release, one of the tracks from this EP included vocals from a mystery girl, who was revealed to be [[Blackpink’s Rosé]] years later.
- [CRAYON (크레용)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t3ULhmadHkg)
#### Kwon Ji Yong
His self-titled album is the last project he released before his enlistment in the military, and it was his last release before Ubermensch, 8 years later. It was a very different musical colour to his previous releases, which is especially visible in Untitled, 2014.
- [무제(無題) (Untitled, 2014)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9kaCAbIXuyg)
