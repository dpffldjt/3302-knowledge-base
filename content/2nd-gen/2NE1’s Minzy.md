## Bio
Minzy was born in 1994 as Gong Minji. Her great aunt is Gong Ok-jin, recognised Korean folk dancer.<sup>1</sup> Minzy participated in dance competitions as a kid, through which she was scouted by [[Seo Taiji and Boys’ Yang Hyun-suk]].

In 2016, Minzy officially left [[YG Entertainment]] and [[2NE1]], before the group officially disbanded. She then signed with Music Works, releasing her solo debut Minzy Work 01. “Uno” in 2017. In 2020, she left Music Works due to legal disputes,<sup>2</sup> and started her own agency called MZ Entertainment.

Minzy rejoined 2NE1 and YG Entertainment for 2NE1’s 15th anniversary reunion in 2024.
