## Bio
Taeyang was born Dong Young-bae in 1988. His stage name, Taeyang, means ‘sun’ in Korean. He became a trainee at [[YG Entertainment]], and originally was training to be in a [duo](https://youtu.be/29HtHS171HI?si=iF4cOV0anWnjMRRj) with [[BigBang’s G-Dragon|G-Dragon]], but plans changed, and they became the first members of the group that later became [[BigBang]], debuting in 2006. 

In 2008, he released his first solo EP with the title track [Prayer (기도)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ETk4-LNuoBs&list=OLAK5uy_kg9PBIfvL4EK61znO8TlEFYa0yk4ccPm0). In 2014, he released his second solo album, including the massively popular track [Eyes, Nose, Lips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UwuAPyOImoI), which was written when he was broken up with his now-wife, actress Min Hyo-rin.

In 2022, Taeyang left YG Entertainment and joined [[THEBLACKLABEL]], affiliate of YG. Since then, he released a few singles, including collaborations with [[BTS’s Jimin]] and [[Blackpink’s Lisa]], and a full album in early 2026.
## Associations
Other than BigBang, he is also married to actress Min Hyo-rin, and they have a child together.
