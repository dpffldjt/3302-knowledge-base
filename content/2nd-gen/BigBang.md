## Bio
BigBang debuted in 2006 under [[YG Entertainment]] with a lineup of five members. [[BigBang’s G-Dragon|G-Dragon]] and [[BigBang’s Taeyang|Taeyang]] were approached first with plans to make a boy group. G-Dragon then contacted childhood friend [[BigBang’s T.O.P|T.O.P]], and [[BigBang’s Seungri|Seungri]] was scouted from a show looking for the “second-generation Shinhwa.” The original lineup also included [[BigBang’s Daesung|Daesung]] and Troublemaker’s Jang Hyun-seung, who was dropped from the group but later debuted in Troublemaker.

Early in their careers, Big Bang released songs like [We Belong Together](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bjTEMBB-mjY) featuring [[2NE1’s Bom]] and formed subunit GD&TOP. They broke through internationally as leaders of the Korean Wave with [Fantastic Baby](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AAbokV76tkU) becoming a major success among both domestic and international audiences. This was followed up by the [MADE album](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nmV9t7lKxIMIdQc7zCPgSYK1jI5AXQwnI) in 2016 after a years-long hiatus<sup>1</sup>.

After the MADE album-associated tours, the members began their enlistment period. In 2019, Seungri leaves the group as a consequence of his involvement with the [[Burning Sun scandal]]. In 2022, T.O.P is the first member to leave YG Entertainment<sup>2</sup>, with Daesung and Taeyang soon following<sup>3</sup>. In 2023, T.O.P leaves the group permanently, citing his worry that his drug scandal would harm the group’s reputation<sup>4</sup>. Later that year, G-Dragon leaves the label and Big Bang is no longer directly managed under YG<sup>5</sup>.

In 2026, remaining members [[BigBang’s G-Dragon|G-Dragon]], [[BigBang’s Taeyang|Taeyang]], and [[BigBang’s Daesung|Daesung]] performed at Coachella and are touring for their 20th anniversary.
