## Bio
T.O.P, born Choi Seunghyun in 1987, was the third member of [[BigBang]] to be chosen, and originally the oldest. He was childhood friends with [[BigBang’s G-Dragon]], having grown up in the same neighbourhood.

He joined BigBang after [[Yang Hyun-suk]] approached G-Dragon and [[BigBang’s Taeyang|Taeyang]] about making an idol group, and G-Dragon called him to ask him to audition. Though he was initially rejected based on his appearance, he was accepted on his second audition.<sup>1</sup>

While in BigBang, he began working on individual projects, like acting and solo music. He released a single, [Doom Dada](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tAoME_aMm1w) and had roles in films like Commitment (2013). This lasted until he enlisted, during which time he became embroiled in a drug use scandal. He was prosecuted for using marijuana, and evidence was later found of abuse of benzodiazepine.<sup>2</sup>

Due to concerns of backlash toward the group, T.O.P first left [[YG Entertainment]] in 2022, then confirmed he had left the group in 2023.<sup>3</sup> He has, however, continued his acting career, starring in Squid Game season 2 as Thanos. He also continued working on solo music, releasing an album in 2026.
